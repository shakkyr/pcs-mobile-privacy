Privacy Policy — Point Cloud Studio Mobile

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Point Cloud Studio Mobile ("the app") is a point cloud viewer for Android. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

The short version

The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything happens on your device.

What the app accesses

  • Files you open. When you choose a point cloud file (LAS, LAZ, PLY, XYZ, TXT, CSV, PTS), the app reads it only to display it. The file is read on your device and is never uploaded anywhere.
  • Points you pick. The names and coordinates of points you pick are stored on your device so they are still there when you reopen a file. They are never sent off the device.
  • Exported CSV files. When you export, the app writes a CSV file to your device's Documents folder and lets you share it using Android's own share menu. You choose where it goes. The app itself does not send it anywhere.

What the app does NOT do

  • It does not collect analytics or usage data.
  • It does not contain advertising.
  • It does not create an account or ask you to sign in.
  • It does not access your location, contacts, camera, or microphone.
  • It does not connect to the internet to send your data. (The app declares the INTERNET permission only because the Android WebView component requires it; no user data is transmitted.)

Data storage and deletion

All app data (your picked points and settings) is stored locally. Uninstalling the app removes it. CSV files you exported stay in your Documents folder until you delete them yourself.

Children

The app is a professional surveying tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL and the date above will change.
